Font Size
This is likely quite obvious, but one of the best ways to make your website more accessible is by having a larger font. It's reccommended to have around 20 pixel font size.
Contrast
You should try and have a high contrast between text, and your background such as: white-black, red-blue, white-dark blue

Don't place text on images, unless you put it on a solid color.
