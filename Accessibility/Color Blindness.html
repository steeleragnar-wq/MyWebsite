The Types of Color Blindess
There are 4 types of color vision
Trichromatic
Normal Vision
Anomalous Trichromacy
Within this catagory there are 3 types of color blindness:
-Protanomaly (reduced sensitivity to red light)
-Deuteranomaly (reduced sensitivity to green light)
-Tritanomaly (reduced sensitivity to blue light light)
Dichromatic
Within this catagory there are another 3 types of color blindness:
-Protanopia (inability to percieve red light)
-Deuterannopia (inability to percieve green light)
-Tritanopia (inability to percieve blue light)
Monochromatic
Within this catagory there are 2 types of color blindness:
-Monochromacy/Achromatopsia (Complete color blindness)
-Blue Cone Monochromacy (Poor color vision)
Avoid These Color Combanations
Certain color combanations are difficult to see for people with colorblindness, avoid the following color combanations:
  • Green-Red
  • Green-Blue
  • Green-Black
  • Green-Brown
  • Green-Gray
  • Light Green-Yellow
  • Blue-Purple
  • Blue-Gray
