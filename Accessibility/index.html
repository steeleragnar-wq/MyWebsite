Website Accessibility Barriers
There are many barriers disabled people may face when trying to interact with a website. These may include inability to read due to contrast, inibility to understand media due to subtitles and more, these are some of the biggest issues and possible solutions.
Contrast
-Poor color contrast can be difficult to read for individuals with visual imparment, one way to check your website's color contrast is with a color contrast checker (Link)
Lack of Captions
Images, videos, and audio clips should have captitons for people with visual, or hearing imparment
Mouse only Navigation
People with motor/physical disibilities, may find it difficult, or impossible to navigate websites with a mouse, or trackpad
